Phase 53E — Shuffle Animation Final Tuning & Regression
========================================================

INSPECTED (Phase 53D baseline)
------------------------------
- ShuffleBooster: logical shuffle → AnimateShuffleMove per cell → FinalizeShufflePresentation
- WorldPieceMotion.AnimateShuffleMove: single ShuffleId tween (anticipate/travel/settle)
- Stagger: 0.018s per Block unit; chain cells launch together per Block
- PieceView3D: squash via SetPresentationAnticipation; nested inner follows outer mesh scale

ISSUES FOUND
------------
1. Squash envelope had value discontinuities at anticipate→travel and travel→settle boundaries
   (peak 0.14 → 0.128 → 0.031 → 0.039 jump), causing subtle “rubbery stop/start” feel.
2. FinalizeShufflePresentation called SetGridPosition without preserveWorldPresentation,
   forcing SyncWorldViewPosition after tween completion — potential visible snap at destination.
3. peakSquash 0.14 and lift 3.2% were slightly strong for premium mobile feel.
